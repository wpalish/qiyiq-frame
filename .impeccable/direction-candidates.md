# QiyiqFrame seven grounded directions — before roll

Mechanism: compare one source with a numerically defined PNG, retaining an exact source-bound recipe. Scene: a local photo/content worker preparing an owned synthetic photograph for a separate later review. Cultural home: graphic production, publication and visual evidence. First surface must show exactly which pixels are kept and what is added. Rut: black photo-tool control panel, and its predictable white upload wizard opposite; neither is a candidate. Literal frame/ruler metaphor also stays out of the list.

1. Modern photographic monograph. Warm paper, ink black, muted rust; editorial sans labels and a restrained serif caption. Full paired source/result spread above a measured caption strip; input controls use native fields. Resonates with checking a delivered photograph, not tool cosplay. Risk: apparent simplicity can bury necessary bounds.
2. Print-shop job ticket. Uncoated pale stock, indigo ink, one red exception; sans paragraphs and monospaced measurements. One task sheet with numbered source/geometry/output columns and frozen recipe stubs. Resonates with explicit production specifications; risk paperwork density.
3. Gallery exhibition labels. Chalk white, graphite, mineral blue; large quiet sans and compact caption labels. Image pair occupies the wall; measurements and export are a continuous lower rail. Resonates with careful visual comparison; risk too much unused area on a laptop.
4. Railway wayfinding standard. Warm grey, near-black, saturated yellow as active state; robust sans with large aligned numerals. Source → geometry → PNG stages share a strict sign grid, no decorative train imagery. Resonates with knowing current stage and next action; risk a transit costume if symbols outweigh task.
5. Library facsimile catalogue. Pale ivory, dark green, black type; serif descriptions and sturdy sans controls. Visual comparison leads, then indexed frozen recipes with source fingerprints. Resonates with retrieving exactly the same item; risk archive may dominate editing.
6. Swiss technical publication. White, black, signal red, precise type scales and aligned footnotes. Two image columns, one dense specification column, flat uninterrupted page. Resonates with controlled reproduction; risk category familiarity.
7. Packaging dieline proof sheet. Light neutral stock, plum text, cyan measurement emphasis; plain sans and numerical captions. Output panel plus a small source proof, control rows clearly separate safe geometry from snapshot metadata. Resonates with checking a single production proof; risk over-technical terminology. This direction uses proof-sheet organization, never a fake operating instrument.
